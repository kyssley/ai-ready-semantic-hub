# Community conduct / 社区行为准则

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome contributors regardless of experience or background. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Use synthetic examples and protect customer and colleague confidentiality. Give attribution where required by third-party licenses. Disclose conflicts of interest when recommending commercial services.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, warn participants or restrict participation, proportionate to the behavior. To report conduct concerns, request a private maintainer contact without publishing personal information. Apply the same standards in issues, pull requests, discussions and project-associated events.

请尊重不同背景的参与者，以事实和可复现案例讨论问题，避免人身攻击。不得公开客户数据、凭证或个人隐私。维护者可按违规程度提醒、移除内容或限制参与。
